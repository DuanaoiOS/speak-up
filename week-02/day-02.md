# Week 2 Day 2: 解释与举例

> 今日目标：学会把抽象观点用具体例子"撑起来"，而不是只说空话

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：What I mean by that is [simpler explanation].

**用在哪**：说完一个比较抽象的观点后，立刻用简单的话解释。这是好表达者的习惯。

**例句**：I think the education system is broken. What I mean by that is it was designed for a world that no longer exists — it teaches kids to memorize, not to think.

**轮到你**：
1. "I think people should travel more." → What I mean by that is _____.
2. "Social media is changing how we think." → What I mean by that is _____.
3. "Work-life balance is a myth." → What I mean by that is _____.
4. "English speaking is a different skill from English reading." → What I mean by that is _____.

### 句型 2：A good example of this is [specific case].

**用在哪**：用一个具体的例子支撑你的观点。具体的例子 > 抽象的道理。

**例句**：Most people overestimate what they can do in a day and underestimate what they can do in a year. A good example of this is learning a language — you won't see progress in one day, but after a year of daily practice, the difference is massive.

**轮到你**：
1. "Technology makes life easier but also more complicated." → A good example of this is _____.
2. "The best ideas often come from unexpected places." → A good example of this is _____.
3. "Sometimes doing less is actually doing more." → A good example of this is _____.
4. 你自己的观点：_____ → A good example of this is _____.

### 句型 3：It's like [analogy] — [explain the connection].

**用在哪**：用类比让复杂概念瞬间变清晰。好的类比比 10 分钟解释都管用。

**例句**：Passive vocabulary is like having a huge library in your head but no catalog — you know the books are there, but you can't find them when you need them.

**轮到你**：
1. "Learning to speak a language is like **learning to drive — at first you think about every move, but eventually it becomes automatic.**"
2. "A good conversation is like **playing catch — you throw the ball, they catch it and throw it back.**"
3. "Social media algorithms are like _____."
4. 你自己的类比：_____ is like _____.

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Is university education still worth it in 2026?

先说你的观点，然后用"What I mean by that is..."解释，再用"A good example of this is..."举例，最后用一个类比收尾。完整结构：**观点 → 解释 → 例子 → 类比**。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

I think... / What I mean by that is... / A good example of this is... / It's like...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。目标是让一个不了解这个话题的人听完觉得"你说的我懂了"。

**回听**：
- [ ] 是先说了观点再解释，还是上来就解释？（应该先亮观点）
- [ ] 例子是具体的还是泛泛的？（要有画面感）
- [ ] 类比能不能让人"秒懂"？

**修正**：把类比那块重说 3 遍——类比是最难但也是最有说服力的部分。
