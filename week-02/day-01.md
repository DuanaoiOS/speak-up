# Week 2 Day 1: 同意与不同意

> 今日目标：不只是 "I agree" / "I disagree"，学会有层次地表达同意和反对

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：I totally agree. I mean, [add your own point].

**用在哪**：表达同意 + 补充自己的观点。光说 "I agree" 会让对话死掉——加上自己的观点对话才能继续。

**例句**：I totally agree. I mean, it's not just about the money — it's about whether you feel valued.

**轮到你**（对以下观点表达同意并补充你的看法）：
1. "Social media is making us more lonely." → I totally agree. I mean, _____.
2. "English education in China focuses too much on tests." → I totally agree. I mean, _____.
3. "Working from home is actually more productive." → I totally agree. I mean, _____.
4. 对最近别人跟你说的一句话：I totally agree. I mean, _____.

### 句型 2：I see what you mean, but [counter-point].

**用在哪**：委婉地不同意。先承认对方有道理，再提出不同意见。这是最安全的反对方式。

**例句**：I see what you mean about saving money, but sometimes it's worth spending more for better quality.

**轮到你**：
1. "AI will replace most jobs." → I see what you mean, but _____.
2. "People should get married before 30." → I see what you mean, but _____.
3. "Kids shouldn't have smartphones." → I see what you mean, but _____.
4. 你自己的例子：I see what you mean about _____, but _____.

### 句型 3：That's a fair point, although I'd say [your perspective].

**用在哪**：更正式/更有说服力地表达不同意。先承认对方观点合理，然后陈述自己的立场。

**例句**：That's a fair point about the cost, although I'd say the long-term benefits outweigh the initial investment.

**轮到你**：
1. "University education is not worth it anymore." → That's a fair point, although I'd say _____.
2. "Learning a language as an adult is too difficult." → That's a fair point, although I'd say _____.
3. "Money is the most important factor in a job." → That's a fair point, although I'd say _____.
4. 你自己的观点碰撞：That's a fair point, although I'd say _____.

## 二、搭配爆破（10 分钟）

| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 我完全同意 | I couldn't agree more. |
| 某种程度上是这样 | That's true to some extent. / To a certain degree, yes. |
| 我部分同意 | I partly agree. / I'm on the fence about this. |
| 我不太确定 | I'm not so sure about that. |
| 我有不同看法 | I have a different take on this. |
| 从一个角度看...从另一个角度看... | On the one hand... on the other hand... |
| 这取决于具体情况 | It depends on the situation. / It's not that black and white. |
| 我理解你的出发点 | I see where you're coming from. |
| 恕我直言 | With all due respect... |
| 你说的有道理 | You have a point. / That makes sense. |

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Social media does more harm than good. Do you agree or disagree?

选一个立场（完全同意/部分同意/不同意），用今天学的句型表达你的观点。注意：要承认对方立场有合理之处，再说你的观点。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

I totally agree / I see what you mean, but / That's a fair point, although / On the one hand... on the other hand...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。目标：有层次地表达立场，不是只喊口号。

**回听**：
- [ ] 有没有先承认对方有道理再提反对意见？
- [ ] 语气像在"争论"还是"讨论"？（讨论更好）
- [ ] 用了几个今天的句型？

**修正**：重说那段反对的部分，确保听起来"礼貌但坚定"。
