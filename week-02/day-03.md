# Week 2 Day 3: 对比与比较

> 今日目标：有层次地对比和分析两个事物，不只是 "A is better than B"

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：Compared to [A], [B] is [comparison].

**用在哪**：直接对比两个事物，突出差异。比 "A is... but B is..." 更紧凑。

**例句**：Compared to 10 years ago, life today is way more convenient but also way more distracting.

**轮到你**：
1. Compared to **10 years ago**, life today is _____.
2. Compared to **reading English**, speaking English is _____.
3. Compared to **my parents' generation**, my generation is _____.
4. Compared to **small cities**, big cities are _____.
5. 你自己的对比：Compared to _____, _____ is _____.

### 句型 2：The main difference is [key point]. [A] is [adj], whereas [B] is [adj].

**用在哪**：先点出核心差异，再并列对比。适合回答"What's the difference?"。

**例句**：The main difference is the pace of life. City life is fast-paced and stimulating, whereas country life is slow and peaceful.

**轮到你**：
1. **远程工作 vs 办公室工作** → The main difference is _____. Working from home is _____, whereas working in an office is _____.
2. **线上学习 vs 线下学习** → The main difference is _____. Online learning is _____, whereas in-person learning is _____.
3. **年轻时的学习 vs 成年后的学习** → The main difference is _____. Learning as a kid is _____, whereas learning as an adult is _____.
4. 你自己的对比：The main difference between _____ and _____ is _____.

### 句型 3：They're similar in that [commonality], but [key difference].

**用在哪**：先承认相似之处，再点出区别。比只说"they're different"更有洞察力。

**例句**：Reading and speaking are similar in that both require vocabulary and grammar, but speaking requires real-time retrieval — there's no time to look up a word mid-sentence.

**轮到你**：
1. **Chinese and English** → They're similar in that _____, but _____.
2. **Working and studying** → They're similar in that _____, but _____.
3. **Movies and books** → They're similar in that _____, but _____.
4. 你自己找一组对比：They're similar in that _____, but _____.

## 二、搭配爆破（10 分钟）

| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 天壤之别 | worlds apart / night and day |
| 差别不大 | there's not much in it / pretty much the same |
| 各有优劣 | each has its pros and cons |
| 本质上不同 | fundamentally different |
| 惊人的相似 | strikingly similar |
| A 远胜于 B | A is far superior to B / B doesn't even come close |
| 视情况而定 | it varies / it depends |
| 不能一概而论 | you can't generalize |
| 相比之下 | by comparison / in contrast |
| 越来越相似 | they're becoming more and more alike |

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Compare your life now to your life 5 years ago. What's changed and what's stayed the same?

用今天的句型做多层次对比：最大的不同是什么？哪些方面更好了？哪些方面变差了？有没有什么没变的？

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

Compared to 5 years ago... / The main difference is... / I used to be... whereas now I'm... / They're similar in that... / The biggest change has been...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。

**回听**：
- [ ] 是对比了多个方面还是只说了"现在更好"？
- [ ] 对比词有没有加重音？（whereas, compared to, the main difference）
- [ ] 有没有说"不变的"部分？（对比不只是说不同）

**修正**：用 The main difference is... 开头的那句话重说 3 遍。
