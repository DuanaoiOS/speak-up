# Week 1 Day 2: 聊工作与学习

> 今日目标：能用自然的方式介绍你的工作/学习，而不是背职称

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：I work in [field/industry], mostly [verb-ing].

**用在哪**：介绍你做什么，比 "I am a [title]" 更自然。没人关心你的职称，关心你实际做什么。

**例句**：I work in marketing, mostly helping small businesses with their social media.

**轮到你**：
1. I work in **tech**, mostly **writing code for mobile apps.**
2. I work in **education**, mostly **training new teachers.**
3. I work in **finance**, mostly **analyzing investment opportunities.**
4. I work in **healthcare**, mostly **working with elderly patients.**
5. I work in _____ (你的行业), mostly _____ (你实际做什么).

### 句型 2：What that means is [explain in simple terms].

**用在哪**：你说了专业术语或抽象概念后，用这句话用大白话解释一下。这是好的沟通者的习惯。

**例句**：I'm a product manager. What that means is I figure out what features users need and then work with engineers to build them.

**轮到你**（用这个句型解释以下内容）：
1. ...a data analyst. What that means is _____.
2. ...a UX designer. What that means is _____.
3. ...an HR specialist. What that means is _____.
4. 现在解释你自己的工作/专业：What that means is _____

### 句型 3：The thing about [your field] is [interesting observation].

**用在哪**：说出一个关于你行业/专业的"有意思的观察"，比干巴巴介绍有趣得多。

**例句**：The thing about teaching is you end up learning as much as your students do.

**轮到你**：
1. The thing about **working from home** is _____.
2. The thing about **being a student** is _____.
3. The thing about **my industry** is _____.
4. The thing about **learning a language** is _____.
5. 你自己的观察：The thing about _____ is _____.

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Describe what you do — but make it interesting. 

不要只说 "I'm an engineer." 用今天学的句型：你做什么、具体是什么、关于你这行有什么有趣的观察。

假设对方完全不了解你的行业，用 Ta 能听懂的话解释。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

I work in... / What that means is... / The thing about my field is... / On a typical day, I...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。不准停。

**回听**：
- [ ] 用了至少 2 个今天的句型吗？
- [ ] 每个专业词都解释了吗？（用 What that means is...）
- [ ] 语速是自然的还是像在背书？

**修正**：卡壳处重说 3 遍。
