# Week 1 Day 4: 分享经历与见闻

> 今日目标：自然地聊你经历过的事，不像是"背诵课文"

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：I remember this one time when [past situation].

**用在哪**：要讲一个具体的经历了。"One time" 比 "once" 更口语化、更有故事感。

**例句**：I remember this one time when I got completely lost in Tokyo with no phone battery. It was terrifying but also kind of amazing.

**轮到你**：
1. I remember this one time when **I tried to cook for 10 people and...**
2. I remember this one time when **I said the wrong thing in a meeting and...**
3. I remember this one time when **I met someone completely unexpected and...**
4. I remember this one time when **I traveled to _____ and...**
5. I remember this one time when I _____.

### 句型 2：So [situation], right? And then [what happened next].

**用在哪**：讲故事时拉近跟听众的距离。"right?" 让听众参与进来。这是母语者讲故事的核心技巧。

**例句**：So I'm standing there, completely lost, right? And then this old lady comes up to me and starts speaking to me in Japanese, and I'm just nodding like I understand.

**轮到你**（续写故事）：
1. So I walk into the interview, super nervous, right? And then _____.
2. So we're on the plane, about to take off, right? And then _____.
3. So I decide to try cooking this new dish for the first time, right? And then _____.
4. 用自己的经历续写：So _____, right? And then _____.

### 句型 3：It turned out that [unexpected result].

**用在哪**：讲故事的高潮——"结果呢..." 制造转折和意外感。

**例句**：It turned out that the lady wasn't speaking Japanese — she was speaking really fast Korean the whole time.

**轮到你**：
1. I thought I failed the test, but it turned out that _____.
2. I was worried about the presentation, but it turned out that _____.
3. I almost didn't go to that party, but it turned out that _____.
4. 自己的经历：_____, but it turned out that _____.

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Tell a story about something unexpected that happened to you.

可以是旅行中的意外、工作上的乌龙、生活中的巧合。用今天学的句型讲：设定场景 → 发生了什么 → 结果是什么。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

I remember this one time when... / So I'm..., right? And then... / It turned out that... / The best/worst part was... / Looking back... / I'll never forget...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。重要：用口语化的叙述方式，不要像读作文。

**回听**：
- [ ] 听起来像在讲故事还是像在汇报工作？
- [ ] 有没有用 "right?" 这种口语化的拉近方式？
- [ ] 节奏有变化吗？（紧张的地方快一点，关键转折的地方停顿一下）

**修正**：重说 3 遍那个"最有意思的转折部分"。
