# Week 1 Day 1: 让自我介绍会聊天

> 今日目标：告别 "My name is... I'm from..."，学会让人想跟你聊下去的自我介绍方式

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

**大声说。每个替换变体说 3 遍。不出声 = 没练。**

### 句型 1：I've been [verb-ing] for [time] now.

**用在哪**：介绍你做某事的时长——工作、爱好、生活状态。比 "I have done..." 更口语化。

**例句**：I've been working in tech for about 5 years now.

**轮到你**（把划线部分换掉）：
1. I've been **living in this city** for _____ now.
2. I've been **learning English** for _____ now.
3. I've been **doing my current job** for _____ now.
4. I've been **thinking about changing careers** for _____ now.
5. I've been **interested in photography** for _____ now.

### 句型 2：I'm the kind of person who [verb phrase].

**用在哪**：让对方快速了解你的性格/习惯/偏好。比 "I am hardworking" 自然得多。

**例句**：I'm the kind of person who needs my morning coffee before I can function.

**轮到你**：
1. I'm the kind of person who **plans everything in advance.**
2. I'm the kind of person who **can't say no to a good movie.**
3. I'm the kind of person who **gets excited about small things.**
4. I'm the kind of person who **needs alone time to recharge.**
5. I'm the kind of person who **talks to strangers everywhere I go.**

### 句型 3：What I love about [something] is [that clause / noun phrase].

**用在哪**：带热情地介绍你喜欢的事物。比 "I like..." 有感染力。

**例句**：What I love about my job is that no two days are the same.

**轮到你**：
1. What I love about **where I live** is _____.
2. What I love about **my best friend** is _____.
3. What I love about **traveling** is _____.
4. What I love about **learning languages** is _____.
5. What I love about **my hometown** is _____.（用 the fact that / how / the way 引导）

## 三、影子跟读（15 分钟）

**YouTube 搜索**：`"How to introduce yourself naturally in English"`

搜不到就用 `"Tell me about yourself interview answer"` ，找 2-5 分钟的即可。

**跟读方法**：
1. 听一句 → 暂停 → 跟读（模仿语调、重音、停顿）
2. 同一句跟读 3 遍再往下走
3. 最后一遍：不停顿，跟视频同步说（跟不上没关系，尽量跟）

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Introduce yourself like you're at a dinner party meeting someone new. 

不要用 "My name is... I'm from... I work as..." 这种教科书模板。用今天学的句型，做一个**自然的、让人想继续聊下去**的自我介绍。

**准备**（1 分钟，看提示词，不要写稿）：

提示词：what you do / how long / what you're into / something surprising or funny about you

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。中间不要停。说不下去就用最简单的词继续。

**回听**：听录音，诚实回答：
- [ ] 有没有卡壳超过 3 秒的地方？
- [ ] 有没有反复用同一个词（like / very / stuff）？
- [ ] 听起来像是在"读课文"还是"说话"？
- [ ] 3 个句型用了几个？（I've been / I'm the kind of person / What I love about）

**修正**：把卡壳的地方重新说 3 遍。如果根本没用到今天的句型，再录一次。
