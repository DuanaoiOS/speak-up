# Week 1 Day 3: 聊兴趣与热情

> 今日目标：用有感染力的方式聊你真正喜欢的事，不只是 "I like watching movies."

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：I'm really into [something] — like, [elaborate].

**用在哪**：表达热忱。"really into" 比 "like" 强烈，"like" 后面的补充让表达更自然。

**例句**：I'm really into photography — like, I'll spend hours editing a single photo and not even notice the time pass.

**轮到你**：
1. I'm really into **cooking** — like, _____.
2. I'm really into **fitness** — like, _____.
3. I'm really into **reading** — like, _____.
4. I'm really into **music** — like, _____.
5. I'm really into _____ — like, _____.

### 句型 2：I got into [something] because [story/reason].

**用在哪**：说出你怎么开始一个兴趣的——每件事都有个起点，这就是故事。

**例句**：I got into running because I missed my bus one day and had to run 3 kilometers to work. Turns out, I actually enjoyed it.

**轮到你**：
1. I got into **cooking** because _____.
2. I got into **my current hobby** because _____.
3. I got into **the work I do** because _____.
4. I got into **English** because _____.
5. I got into _____ because _____.

### 句型 3：What I find [adj] about it is [observation].

**用在哪**：不只是说"我喜欢"，而是说出"为什么喜欢"——这才是吸引人的部分。

**例句**：What I find fascinating about learning languages is how it changes the way you think — you literally start seeing the world differently.

**轮到你**：
1. What I find **interesting** about **social media** is _____.
2. What I find **frustrating** about **my phone** is _____.
3. What I find **rewarding** about **my hobby** is _____.
4. What I find **fascinating** about **human behavior** is _____.
5. What I find _____ about _____ is _____.

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：What are you really into right now? 

讲一件你最近真正沉迷的事物——可以是一个爱好、一本书、一个剧、一个运动、一个游戏。用今天的句型，讲出**热情感**，不要只列事实。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

I'm really into... / I got into it because... / What I find... about it is... / The best part is... / I could talk about this for hours because...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。目标是让听的人觉得"哇，你这么喜欢，我也想试试"。

**回听**：
- [ ] 听起来有热情吗？还是平得像在读说明书？
- [ ] 你说了"为什么喜欢"吗？还是只说了"是什么"？
- [ ] 有没有用到 I got into... 这个讲故事句型？

**修正**：如果听起来不够有感染力，注意两件事：① 语速有变化（喜欢的地方加快），② 用了具体的例子（不是 it's fun，而是具体哪里 fun）。
