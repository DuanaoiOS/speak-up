# Week 1 Day 5: 表达感受与看法

> 今日目标：自然地表达你的感觉和看法，而不是永远 "I think... I feel happy..."

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：To be honest, I [honest opinion / feeling].

**用在哪**：说出真实的、可能跟别人不一样的想法。"To be honest" 是一个信号——接下来的话是真诚的。

**例句**：To be honest, I wasn't really that excited about it at first. But it grew on me.

**轮到你**：
1. To be honest, I **was a bit disappointed when...**
2. To be honest, I **don't really get the hype about _____ (某个流行事物).**
3. To be honest, I **was pretty nervous about speaking English before I started practicing.**
4. To be honest, I **think _____ is overrated.**
5. To be honest, I _____.

### 句型 2：It feels like [comparison / metaphor].

**用在哪**：用比喻来表达感受，比"it makes me feel..."生动得多。

**例句**：Speaking English for the first time feels like learning to walk again — you know what you want to do, but your body won't cooperate.

**轮到你**：
1. **Starting a new job** feels like **being the new kid at school.**
2. **A long weekend** feels like **a mini vacation for your brain.**
3. **Trying to speak a new language** feels like _____.
4. **Living in [your city]** feels like _____.
5. _____ feels like _____.

### 句型 3：I can't help but feel that [opinion/feeling].

**用在哪**：表达一种你"忍不住这么觉得"的看法。比 "I think" 有情感温度。

**例句**：I can't help but feel that we spend way too much time on our phones and not enough time actually talking to each other.

**轮到你**：
1. I can't help but feel that **time is passing faster every year.**
2. I can't help but feel that **most meetings could have been emails.**
3. I can't help but feel that _____.
4. I can't help but feel that _____.
5. I can't help but think that _____. (think 也可以替换 feel)

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：How do you feel about the way you spend your time these days?

可以是满足的、焦虑的、矛盾的、开心的——重要的是诚实地说出真实的感受，不要给"正确答案"。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

To be honest... / It feels like... / I can't help but feel that... / The thing is... / I guess what I'm trying to say is...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。说出**矛盾的、复杂的感受**——真实的情感从来不是简单的 happy 或 sad。

**回听**：
- [ ] 只说了 I think... I feel... 还是用了更多样的表达？
- [ ] 有没有用具体的比喻或例子来支撑感受？
- [ ] 听起来真诚吗？还是像在说"正确答案"？

**修正**：把最卡的那句话换一种方式说 3 遍。
