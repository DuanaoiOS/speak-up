## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：My background is in [field], specifically [focus area]. What that means in practice is [real-world impact].

**用在哪**：自我介绍。"Tell me about yourself" 的满分回答结构。

**例句**：My background is in marketing, specifically content strategy. What that means in practice is I help companies figure out what to say and how to say it so people actually want to listen.

**轮到你**：
1. My background is in _____, specifically _____. What that means in practice is _____.
2. I've spent the last _____ years working in _____. My main focus has been _____.
3. 你的真实版本：My background is in _____. What that means in practice is _____.

### 句型 2：One challenge I faced was [situation]. What I did was [action]. And the result was [outcome].

**用在哪**：回答行为面试题（"Tell me about a time you..."）。这就是 STAR 法的口语版。

**例句**：One challenge I faced was that our team was consistently missing deadlines. What I did was set up a simple tracking system and a 10-minute daily check-in. The result was we hit every deadline for the next 6 months — and people actually liked the new process.

**轮到你**：
1. 你解决的一个问题：One challenge I faced was _____. What I did was _____. The result was _____.
2. 你跟别人合作有分歧：One challenge I faced was _____. What I did was _____. The result was _____.
3. 你从失败中学到东西：One challenge I faced was _____. What I did was _____. The result was _____. (结果可以是失败，但你学到了什么）

### 句型 3：Where I see myself in [time frame] is [vision]. I'm particularly interested in [specific area] because [reason].

**用在哪**："Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" 不要给空洞的答案。

**例句**：Where I see myself in 5 years is leading a product team. I'm particularly interested in the intersection of technology and education because I've seen firsthand how much impact a well-designed product can have on how people learn.

**轮到你**：
1. Where I see myself in 3-5 years is _____. I'm particularly interested in _____ because _____.
2. I want to build expertise in _____ because I believe _____.
3. 你的真实答案：Where I see myself in 5 years is _____.

## 二、搭配爆破（10 分钟）

| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 我有...年的经验 | I have [X] years of experience in... |
| 我最擅长的是... | my strength is... / what I'm best at is... |
| 我还在提升的地方是... | an area I'm working on is... |
| 我跟团队合作很顺畅 | I work well with cross-functional teams |
| 我主导过一个项目... | I led a project where... / I took the initiative to... |
| 结果超出了预期 | the results exceeded expectations |
| 我对这个职位很感兴趣因为... | I'm drawn to this role because... |
| 这个岗位跟我职业方向很契合 | this role aligns with where I want to take my career |
| 我看重持续成长 | I value continuous growth / I'm always looking to learn |
| 我能为团队带来什么 | what I bring to the table is... |

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**模拟面试**：回答下面 3 个问题，每个回答 1 分钟。当作真实面试，录音。

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem.
3. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

**准备**（每题 1 分钟，可用提示词）：

Q1: My background is in... / What that means in practice is... / I'm the kind of person who...

Q2: One challenge I faced was... / What I did was... / The result was...

Q3: Where I see myself in 5 years is... / I'm particularly interested in... because...

**回听**：
- [ ] 听起来像在背书还是像在对话？（应该是自然对话感）
- [ ] 每个回答都有具体例子吗？还是有套话？
- [ ] Q2 有没有明确的 STAR 结构（情境→行动→结果）？
- [ ] 有没有 filler words (um, uh, like) 太多？

**修正**：最不流畅的那个回答重做，直到 1 分钟内讲清楚。
