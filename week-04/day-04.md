# Week 4 Day 4: 处理问题与投诉

> 今日目标：遇到问题能用英文冷静地应对，包括投诉和被投诉

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：I understand [their feeling / situation]. Here's what I can do: [solution].

**用在哪**：处理别人的投诉/不满——先共情、再说解法。这个顺序不能反。

**例句**：I understand how frustrating this must be. Here's what I can do: I'll process a refund right now and send you a confirmation email within 5 minutes.

**轮到你**：
1. 顾客说菜等太久：I understand _____. Here's what I can do: _____.
2. 同事说你没及时回复消息：I understand _____. Here's what I can do: _____.
3. 朋友抱怨你取消了聚会：I understand _____. Here's what I can do: _____.
4. 你的真实场景：I understand _____. Here's what I can do: _____.

### 句型 2：I'm not happy with [issue]. / There seems to be a problem with [something]. Can we figure this out?

**用在哪**：你作为顾客/用户去投诉或反映问题。坚定但礼貌。

**例句**：Hi, there seems to be a problem with my order — I was charged twice. Can we figure this out?

**轮到你**：
1. 酒店房间有问题：I'm not happy with **the room — it's not what I booked.** Can we _____?
2. 账单不对：There seems to be a problem with **the bill. It has items I didn't order.** Can we _____?
3. 服务不满意：I wanted to bring something to your attention. _____. What can we do about this?
4. 你自己遇到过的问题：There seems to be a problem with _____. Can we _____?

### 句型 3：Let me look into that and get back to you by [specific time].

**用在哪**：你暂时解决不了，需要调查后再回复。关键是给一个具体的时间承诺。

**例句**：I don't have the answer right now, but let me look into that and get back to you by end of day tomorrow.

**轮到你**：
1. 同事问你一个你答不上来的问题：Let me look into that and get back to you by _____.
2. 客户问一个技术问题：I'll need to check with the team on that. Let me get back to you by _____.
3. 朋友问你能不能帮一个你不知道能不能帮的忙：Let me _____ and get back to you by _____.

## 二、搭配爆破（10 分钟）

| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 这不应该发生的 | this shouldn't have happened / this is not the experience we want you to have |
| 我来帮你处理 | let me take care of this for you / I'll handle this |
| 我理解你的不满 | I understand your frustration / I can see why you'd be upset |
| 我们会尽快解决 | we'll get this sorted out as soon as possible |
| 给你带来不便，抱歉 | sorry for the inconvenience / I apologize for the trouble |
| 这不是借口，但是... | I'm not making excuses, but... / not to make excuses, but... |
| 你希望我怎么处理？ | how would you like me to handle this? / what would work best for you? |
| 我马上就办 | I'm on it / I'll take care of it right away |
| 我帮你跟进 | I'll follow up on this / let me chase that up |
| 这样解决你满意吗？ | does that work for you? / are you happy with this solution? |

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**模拟场景**：你在一家餐厅，等了 40 分钟菜还没上。你跟服务员反映。

服务员说：厨房太忙了，没办法。你继续沟通。

完整对话，录音。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

Excuse me, I wanted to bring something to your attention. / We've been waiting for... / I understand..., but... / What can we do about this? / Is there anything you can do? / OK, thank you. I appreciate it.

**开始**：录音，1-2 分钟。

**回听**：
- [ ] 开场用了"I understand..."先共情吗？
- [ ] 语气是"抱怨"还是"解决问题"？（后者更好）
- [ ] 有没有提出具体的解决方案？
- [ ] 如果服务员说"No"，你有跟进吗？

**修正**：如果语气听起来太冲或太弱，调整后重说 3 遍。
