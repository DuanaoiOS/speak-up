# Week 4 Day 5: 社交场合

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：How do you know [host / person]? / What brings you here?

**用在哪**：社交场合最好的开场白。自然、不生硬、给了对方说话的方向。

**例句**：So how do you know David? / What brings you to this event?

**轮到你**：
1. 在一个朋友组织的聚会上认识新朋友：So how do you know _____?
2. 在一个行业活动上：What brings you to this conference?
3. 在一个婚礼上：Are you on the bride's side or the groom's side?
4. 你的版本：So _____? / What brings you _____?

### 句型 2：That reminds me of [story / thought]. / Speaking of [topic], [related point].

**用在哪**：顺着话题延展，不让对话死掉。这是社交高手的核心技巧。

**例句**：That reminds me of a place I went to in Chengdu — the food was incredible, but I couldn't handle the spice. / Speaking of travel, have you been anywhere interesting lately?

**轮到你**：
1. 对方说"我很喜欢做饭" → That reminds me of _____. / Speaking of cooking, _____.
2. 对方说"我最近在找工作" → That reminds me of _____. / Speaking of career changes, _____.
3. 对方说了一个你去过的地方 → That reminds me of _____. / Speaking of _____, _____.
4. 你自己顺着话题延伸：_____. That reminds me of _____. / Speaking of _____, _____.

### 句型 3：Anyway, [transition]. / I should probably [go / mingle / get another drink]. It was great talking to you!

**用在哪**：优雅地结束一段对话。不要突兀地走开，先给一个过渡信号。

**例句**：Anyway, I should probably let you grab some food — I've been talking your ear off. It was really great meeting you!

**轮到你**：
1. Anyway, I should probably **go find my friend — I promised I'd call them.** It was great talking to you!
2. Anyway, I'm going to **grab another drink.** It was really nice chatting with you!
3. I don't want to take up your whole evening. I'll let you go mingle. It was great meeting you!
4. 你的优雅退场：Anyway, _____. It was great _____.

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**模拟场景**：你在一个聚会上，走向一个你不认识的人，开始聊天。聊 2-3 轮，然后自然结束对话。

给对方一个身份：做设计的、刚从国外回来的、看起来有点无聊的、正在吃很多东西的——你根据这个来开场和聊天。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

How do you know [host]? / What brings you here? / That reminds me of... / Tell me more about that. / Speaking of... / Anyway, I should probably... / It was great talking to you!

**开始**：录音，1-2 分钟。要求：开场自然、话题延续、退场不尴尬。

**回听**：
- [ ] 开场有没有让对方很容易回答？
- [ ] 有没有顺着对方说的话延展话题（That reminds me / speaking of）？
- [ ] 结束对话时有过渡还是太突然？
- [ ] 整体感觉像是在"做任务"还是像在"聊天"？

**修正**：如果开场太生硬，换一种开场方式再录一遍。
