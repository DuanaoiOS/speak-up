# Week 3 Day 4: 变化与转折

> 今日目标：自然地讲述人生中的变化、转折点和"之前vs之后"

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：I used to [old habit/belief], but now I [new behavior/perspective].

**用在哪**：讲个人变化。*used to* 是口语中最常用的表达"过去曾经"的方式。

**例句**：I used to think being busy meant being important. But now I realize that being busy just means... being busy. It has nothing to do with doing meaningful work.

**轮到你**：
1. I used to **hate exercising**, but now I _____.
2. I used to **be afraid of speaking up in meetings**, but now I _____.
3. I used to **think success meant _____,** but now I think _____.
4. 你的真实变化：I used to _____, but now I _____.

### 句型 2：Everything changed when [specific event or realization].

**用在哪**：点出改变的催化剂——那一件事或那个瞬间。

**例句**：Everything changed when my manager said to me, "You're the quietest person in the room, but you have the best ideas. Why don't you share them?" That hit me hard.

**轮到你**：
1. Everything changed when **I read this one book that said _____.**
2. Everything changed when **I met someone who _____.**
3. Everything changed when **I failed at _____ and realized _____.**
4. 你人生的转折点：Everything changed when _____.

### 句型 3：Looking back, I realize [retrospective insight].

**用在哪**：事后反思——"当时不觉得，现在回头看才明白"。

**例句**：Looking back, I realize that getting fired from that job was the best thing that ever happened to me. It forced me to figure out what I actually wanted.

**轮到你**：
1. Looking back, I realize **that decision I was so worried about didn't matter that much.**
2. Looking back, I realize **my parents were right about _____.**
3. Looking back, I realize **the hardest periods in my life were also the ones where I grew the most.**
4. 你的反思：Looking back, I realize _____.

## 二、搭配爆破（10 分钟）

| 中文 |英文 |
|------|------|
| 180 度大转变 | a complete 180 / a total turnaround |
| 转折点 | a turning point / a tipping point |
| 打开了我的眼界 | it opened my eyes / it was eye-opening |
| 彻底改变了我的人生轨迹 | it completely changed the course of my life |
| 从一个极端到另一个极端 | from one extreme to the other |
| 之前和之后的差别太大了 | the before and after is night and day |
| 我花了很多年才改掉这个习惯 | it took me years to break that habit |
| 老的我会...现在的我会... | the old me would have... / now I'd... |
| 成长是不舒服的 | growth is uncomfortable |
| 不进则退 | if you're not growing, you're shrinking |

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：What's one big way you've changed in the last few years?

可以是性格、习惯、价值观、生活方式的变化。用到 I used to... Everything changed when... Looking back... 的完整弧线。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

I used to... / I always thought... / Everything changed when... / At first it was... but then... / Looking back, I realize... / The old me would have... but now I...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。讲出一个有"转折感"的故事——变化之前和变化之后要有鲜明的对比。

**回听**：
- [ ] used to 和 but now 的对比够鲜明吗？
- [ ] 转折的催化剂（everything changed when）具体吗？还是太模糊？
- [ ] Looking back 的反思有没有深度？

**修正**：Looking back 那句话重说 3 遍，确保有洞察力（不只是"挺好的"级别的反思）。
