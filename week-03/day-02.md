# Week 3 Day 2: 时间顺序

> 今日目标：用丰富的时间连接词把事件串起来，告别 "and then... and then... and then..."

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：At first, [initial situation]. But then [turning point].

**用在哪**：任何有转折的故事。"At first... but then..." 是最基本也最好用的故事结构。

**例句**：At first, everything was going according to plan — we got to the airport on time, the flight was smooth. But then we landed and realized our luggage was in a different country.

**轮到你**：
1. At first, **I didn't think much of the opportunity.** But then _____.
2. At first, **learning to speak felt impossible.** But then _____.
3. At first, **I was terrified of public speaking.** But then _____.
4. 你的经历：At first, _____. But then _____.

### 句型 2：Before I knew it, [something happened quickly/unexpectedly].

**用在哪**：表达某事发生得比预期快，或不知不觉中就发生了。

**例句**：Before I knew it, three hours had passed and I was still on that phone call — we talked like old friends.

**轮到你**：
1. Before I knew it, **the deadline was 2 days away and I hadn't started.**
2. Before I knew it, **I had spent 500 yuan on things I didn't need.**
3. Before I knew it, **6 months had passed and I could actually hold a conversation in English.**
4. 你的经历：Before I knew it, _____.

### 句型 3：The next thing I knew, [surprising event — often dramatic].

**用在哪**：强调事件的突然性和戏剧性。比 "and then" 有冲击力得多。

**例句**：I closed my eyes for what felt like one second. The next thing I knew, the sun was up and my phone was buzzing with 15 missed calls.

**轮到你**：
1. I was just walking down the street, minding my own business. The next thing I knew, _____.
2. I sat down to watch one episode before bed. The next thing I knew, _____.
3. I thought the conversation was going fine. The next thing I knew, _____.
4. 你的故事：_____. The next thing I knew, _____.

## 二、搭配爆破（10 分钟）

| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 一开始 | initially / in the beginning |
| 没过多久 | before long / it wasn't long before |
| 与此同时 | meanwhile / in the meantime |
| 渐渐地 | gradually / little by little / bit by bit |
| 突然之间 | all of a sudden / out of nowhere |
| 就在那时 | just then / right at that moment |
| 最终/到头来 | eventually / in the end |
| 一晃（时间）过去了 | before I knew it, [time] had passed |
| 等到...的时候 | by the time... |
| 从那以后 | ever since then / from that point on |

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Tell me about your first day at something — a job, a school, a trip, anything where you were the "new person."

用时间顺序讲：开始是怎样的 → 中间发生了什么 → 转折点 → 结果。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

At first... / Before long... / Then, all of a sudden... / The next thing I knew... / By the time... / Eventually...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。挑战：不用 "and then" 超过 2 次。

**回听**：
- [ ] 数一下说了几次 "and then"？超过 2 次了吗？
- [ ] 时间过渡词多样化了吗？
- [ ] 节奏有没有变化？（快的事件→快语速，紧张的时刻→停顿）

**修正**：把 "and then" 的地方用今天的词替换，重说 3 遍。
