# Week 3 Day 3: 细节与感受

> 今日目标：学会在故事中穿插感受和细节——听众记住的不是事实，是你让他们"感受"到的

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：I remember feeling [emotion] because [reason]. I remember thinking, "[direct thought]".

**用在哪**：把听众拉进你的内心世界。直接引语（你当时脑子里想的那句话）比间接描述有力得多。

**例句**：I remember feeling this wave of panic wash over me. I remember thinking, "This is it. I've really done it this time."

**轮到你**：
1. 关于一个紧张的时刻：I remember feeling _____. I remember thinking, "_____."
2. 关于一个惊喜的时刻：I remember feeling _____. I remember thinking, "_____."
3. 关于一个骄傲的时刻：I remember feeling _____. I remember thinking, "_____."
4. 你的真实经历：I remember feeling _____. I remember thinking, "_____."

### 句型 2：The [sensory detail] that struck me was [specific observation].

**用在哪**：用一个感官细节让场景立体起来——视觉、听觉、嗅觉、触觉。

**例句**：The detail that struck me was how quiet the office was. No keyboards clicking, no phones ringing — just this eerie silence. You could hear someone coughing three floors away.

**轮到你**（给每个情境加一个具体的感官细节）：
1. **走进一个陌生的地方**：The thing that struck me was how _____.
2. **见到一个很久没见的人**：The thing that struck me was how _____.
3. **到一个新的城市**：The thing that struck me was the smell/sound/sight of _____.
4. 你的经历：The thing that struck me was _____.

### 句型 3：That's when I realized [insight or shift in understanding].

**用在哪**：故事的"顿悟时刻"——一切从这里开始改变。

**例句**：That's when I realized I wasn't nervous about speaking English — I was nervous about sounding stupid. And those are two completely different things.

**轮到你**：
1. That's when I realized **this job wasn't for me.**
2. That's when I realized **I had been worrying about the wrong thing all along.**
3. That's when I realized **_____ wasn't the problem — _____ was the problem.**
4. 你的顿悟时刻：That's when I realized _____.

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Tell me about a time you felt really proud of yourself.

用今天的句型讲述：当时的场景是怎样的 → 你具体感受到了什么（身体感受+情绪） → 你的"顿悟时刻"是什么。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

I remember feeling... / I remember thinking... / The detail that struck me was... / My heart was... / That's when I realized... / It was all worth it.

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。目标：让听众不只知道你骄傲，而是能**感受到**你的骄傲。

**回听**：
- [ ] 有没有说"I remember thinking" + 一个具体的内心独白？
- [ ] 有没有身体感受的描述？（心跳、呼吸、表情）
- [ ] 有没有一个"顿悟时刻"？

**修正**：把"I remember thinking"那句重说 3 遍，把内心独白讲得更生动。
