# Week 3 Day 1: 搭建场景

> 今日目标：学会给故事搭建场景——时间、地点、人物、氛围，让听的人"看见"画面

---

## 一、句型框架（15 分钟）

### 句型 1：This goes back to [time] when I was [situation].

**用在哪**：故事的开场。"Goes back to" 比 "happened in" 更有故事感。

**例句**：This goes back to about 3 years ago when I was living in Shanghai and didn't really know what I wanted to do with my life.

**轮到你**：
1. This goes back to **when I was in college**. I was _____.
2. This goes back to **my first job**. I had just _____.
3. This goes back to **a trip I took to _____**. I was traveling alone and _____.
4. 你的一个真实故事：This goes back to _____ when I was _____.

### 句型 2：Picture this: [vivid scene description].

**用在哪**：用一句话帮听众"看到"场景。故事高手都用这招。

**例句**：Picture this: it's 11 PM on a Tuesday, I'm in a tiny hotel room in a city I don't know, my phone just died, and I have no idea where my passport is.

**轮到你**（给下面每个情境加 2-3 个具体画面细节）：
1. Picture this: **your first day at a new job.** It's... You're... The room is...
2. Picture this: **a busy train station during Spring Festival.** People are... The air is... You can hear...
3. Picture this: **your most embarrassing moment.** You're standing there... Everyone is looking at you... Your face feels...
4. 你自己的故事：Picture this: _____. It's _____. You're _____. You can hear/smell/feel _____.

### 句型 3：The thing you have to understand is [backstory].

**用在哪**：讲故事前给必要的背景信息，让听众明白"为什么这很重要"。

**例句**：The thing you have to understand is, I had never traveled alone before this trip. I was the kind of person who always had a plan, always had a backup. So when I found myself completely lost...

**轮到你**：
1. 为什么决定换工作：The thing you have to understand is, _____.
2. 为什么那天特别重要：The thing you have to understand is, _____.
3. 关于你要讲的故事：The thing you have to understand is, _____.

## 二、搭配爆破（10 分钟）

| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 事情是这样的 | here's the thing / so here's what happened |
| 当时我完全不知道 | at the time, I had no idea... / little did I know... |
| 一切始于 | it all started when... |
| 说巧不巧 | as luck would have it / it just so happened that... |
| 本来没打算 | I wasn't planning on it, but... / it wasn't supposed to happen |
| 那是一个炎热的夏日/寒冷的冬夜 | it was a scorching summer day / a freezing winter night |
| 我记得很清楚 | I remember it vividly / it's still fresh in my memory |
| 空气中弥漫着... | the air was filled with... / you could smell... |
| 氛围很... | the atmosphere was... / there was this feeling of... |
| 像是在电影里一样 | it felt like something out of a movie |

## 三、影子跟读（15 分钟）

**YouTube 搜索**：`"The Moth storytelling"` 或 `"How to start a story in English"`

The Moth 是真人在台上讲真实故事。找一段 3-5 分钟的，注意他们怎么在 30 秒内用具体的场景描述抓住你的注意力。

## 四、即兴表达（15 分钟）

**话题**：Tell me about a moment in your life that felt like a movie scene.

找一个你经历的、有画面感的时刻。用 Picture this 和 The thing you have to understand is 搭建场景。

**准备**（1 分钟，提示词）：

This goes back to... / The thing you have to understand is... / Picture this: ... / The atmosphere was... / I remember thinking...

**开始**：录音，说 1-2 分钟。关键是前 30 秒要让人"看到画面"。

**回听**：
- [ ] 前 30 秒有没有具体的画面细节？（时间、地点、光线、声音？）
- [ ] 听众知道为什么"这个时刻很重要"吗？
- [ ] 用了几种感官？（视觉永远是主导，加一点听觉或触觉更生动）

**修正**：重新说第一段（开场 30 秒），加入更多感官细节。
